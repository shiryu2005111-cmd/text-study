📚 Text Study — Paste & Learn

Paste any text — lyrics, articles, scripts → tap a word for the dictionary, or select any part to study it.
How it works: Copy any text yourself — song lyrics (e.g. from Spotify's lyrics view), news articles, textbook passages, scripts — and paste it here. It's your own private copy, saved only in this browser (localStorage; nothing is uploaded except the part you look up). Tap a word or select a phrase = the free dictionary shows instantly (¥0). If that's not enough, press Shift+W to ask the AI for the meaning in context, Shift+S to paste reference + target text for Claude sentence help, or Shift+T for Japanese translation in the panel (¥0). Select across multiple lines = press Shift+W for AI meaning, Shift+S for the paste form, or Shift+T for Japanese translation. Marker shortcuts: press 1 for Marker ON, 2 for Marker OFF. OFF still searches selected text, but does not add yellow marks. In the dictionary panel, use Pronounce / Slow for sound, tap unknown words or select phrases inside the explanation, then press Back to return. You can also drag the marker over text inside the result panel to highlight it in yellow. Highlights reset when you reload — the act of marking is the point.
